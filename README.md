# 🛒 Amazon Store Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Amazon Store Sales Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI data analytics project** designed to analyze sales, profit, orders, products, customer segments, payment methods, shipping modes, product categories, and regional performance.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, filters, and geographical analysis.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Track orders and total products.
* Understand sales performance across customer segments.
* Analyze different payment and shipping methods.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Monitor sales trends over time.
* Compare quarterly sales and profit.
* Analyze regional and geographical sales performance.
* Create an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides important business KPIs such as:

* 💰 Total Sales
* 📈 Total Profit
* 📦 Total Orders
* 🛍️ Total Products

### 🔹 Sales Analysis

Sales are analyzed based on:

* Customer Segment
* Payment Mode
* Shipping Mode
* Product Category
* Region
* Time Period

### 🔹 Time-Series Analysis

The dashboard provides sales trends across:

**Year → Quarter → Month → Day**

This helps identify changes and spikes in sales performance over time.

### 🔹 Sales vs Profit Analysis

A quarterly comparison of sales and profit helps understand business performance and profitability trends.

### 🔹 Category Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales across major categories including:

* Office Supplies
* Furniture
* Technology

### 🔹 Geographical Analysis

A map visualization is used to understand sales distribution across different geographical locations.
